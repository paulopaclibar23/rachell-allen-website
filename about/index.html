About Rachell Allen

Empowering candidate nurses worldwide to achieve licensure success.

Our History

Rachell Allen Review Services was founded to address the critical gaps in preparation for national licensure nursing exams. Over the last 15 years, we have helped thousands of candidate nurses achieve their career dreams in the US and the Philippines.

Our Mission

To empower candidate nurses to confidently pass their licensure examinations by providing innovative, high-impact review courses, comprehensive guidebooks, and compassionate counselor advisement.

Our Vision

To be the global gold standard in nursing board review services, recognized for our exceptional passing rates and student-centric methodology.
