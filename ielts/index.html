Core Modules Covered

L

Listening

Train your ear for multiple accents (British, American, Australian). Master shorthand note-taking and prompt comprehension.

R

Reading

Develop speed-reading, scanning, and skimming techniques to manage tight timelines. Excel in locating direct textual evidence.

W

Writing

Learn precise structures for academic and general tasks. Master data description templates and cohesive essay composition.

S

Build confidence with mock face-to-face evaluations. Expand vocabulary, polish grammar, and eliminate speech hesitations.
