NCLEX Application Assistance

Navigate your international nursing licensure pathway with confidence. We provide step-by-step guidance for credential evaluations and eligibility filings.

The NCLEX Licensing Steps

Step 01

Credentials Evaluation

Your academic transcripts, professional licenses, and experience are validated through recognized institutions such as CGFNS (US), NNAS (Canada), or AHPRA (Australia) to check foreign training equivalence.

  • Evaluation report requests
  • Syllabus translation support
  • University liaison verification
Step 02

Board Eligibility Filing

Submit formal applications directly to the Board of Nursing in your target jurisdiction. The board reviews credential reports to verify you meet the theoretical and clinical nursing education requirements.

  • Board application review & audits
  • Background checks and fingerprinting
  • Licensure fee structure assistance
Step 03

ATT & Pearson VUE Booking

Once the board declares you eligible, register with Pearson VUE. You will receive an Authorization to Test (ATT) letter, allowing you to schedule your actual NCLEX examination date and venue.

  • ATT release status tracking
  • Testing venue selection guidance
  • Rescheduling & booking support

Supported Geographic Boards

We specialize in applications across major global destinations, ensuring alignment with specific local regulatory policies.

USA State Boards

Expert filings for New York, Texas, Illinois, New Mexico, and more. Full SSN bypass support where applicable.

Canada NNAS / Provinces

National Nursing Assessment Service registry and provincial licensing transition (OIIQ, CNO, BCCNM).

Australia AHPRA

Portfolio assessments and preparatory guidelines for the OSCE practical exam and NCLEX-RN transition.
